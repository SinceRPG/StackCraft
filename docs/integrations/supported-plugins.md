# Supported Plugins

StackCraft is built to play nicely with your favorite custom item creation plugins.

Here are the officially supported soft-dependencies:

- **[ItemsAdder](https://itemsadder.devs.beer/)**: Custom blocks, items, hats, mobs, etc.
- **[Oraxen](https://docs.oraxen.com/)**: Fast custom items & block resource pack generator.
- **[MMOItems](https://gitlab.com/phoenix-dvpmt/mmoitems)**: The ultimate custom item creator.
- **[ExecutableItems (SCore)](https://www.spigotmc.org/resources/83070/)**: Add custom items with custom triggers/activators.
- **[Nexo](https://docs.nexomc.com/)**: Modern asset engine for Minecraft.
- **[ItemEdit](https://www.spigotmc.org/resources/40993/)**: In-game NBT editor.
- **[MythicMobs](https://mythiccraft.io/)**: Custom monsters, bosses, and items!

To use any of these, simply ensure the plugin is installed on your server, and use their specific prefix format in your `config.yml`.

> [!TIP]
> Ensure StackCraft loads *after* these plugins by not using PlugMan to load/reload plugins. Restart your server!
